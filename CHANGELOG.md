# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-11-18

### Changed

- Requires the 2.0 of the suite core, which runs on [laravel-privacy](https://github.com/jeffersongoncalves/laravel-privacy), the engine shared by the privacy suites. No change to this package's API, config or tables.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-18

First release of laravel-quebec-pia: privacy impact assessments (EFVP) under Quebec Law 25 for information system projects (s. 3.3), before communicating outside Quebec (s. 17) and for research (s. 21), with scored risks, the person in charge's opinion, approval, yearly review and candidates from the governance inventory. Part of the Quebec Law 25 suite for Laravel.
